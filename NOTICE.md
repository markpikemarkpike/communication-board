## The pictures on the tiles

Apart from the one screenshot described below, the pictures are **not** part of this repo. They are **not** covered by the MIT License. The board loads them from ARASAAC when it runs.

> The pictographic symbols used are the property of the Government of Aragón and have been created by Sergio Palao for ARASAAC (http://www.arasaac.org), that distributes them under Creative Commons License BY-NC-SA.

What that means in practice, from ARASAAC's [terms of use](https://arasaac.org/terms-of-use):

- You can use them for non-profit purposes if you credit them as above.
- You cannot use them in a commercial product or publication without written permission from the owner.
- Anything made from them, such as a printed board, carries the same BY-NC-SA license and the same credit.

ARASAAC's terms name the license without a version number. Its Open Classroom site links to [version 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).

The board shows the credit under the tiles, in Settings, and on printed copies. If you fork this project, please keep it there.

If you want to build something commercial on this code, the MIT License lets you. You will need a different set of pictures.
